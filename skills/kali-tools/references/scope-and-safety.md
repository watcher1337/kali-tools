# Scope and safety gate

Record the written authorization reference, exact hosts/URLs/ranges, permitted protocols and techniques, timing/rate/concurrency ceilings, exclusions, test window, approved credentials, data handling, and escalation/stop contact before execution.

- Treat discoveries as observations, not scope expansion.
- Obtain a separate approval before credential spraying, intrusive scripts, state changes, exploitation, remote execution, callbacks, denial-of-service testing, or data retrieval beyond the minimum indicator.
- Stop on instability, unexpected sensitive data, lockout indicators, or conflicting instructions.
